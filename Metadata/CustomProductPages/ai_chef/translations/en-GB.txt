Freshli's Rescue Chef is built on Apple's iOS 26 Foundation Models Framework — the same on-device LLM that powers Apple Intelligence. It reads your exact pantry and writes recipes that use what you have. Right now. With no internet. With nothing leaving your phone.

THIS IS NOT CHATGPT FOR FOOD.
No OpenAI. No Anthropic. No third-party cloud. No tokens shipped to a server. The model runs on your iPhone's Neural Engine — instantly, privately, even on aeroplane mode. Your food data is yours. Forever.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Open Freshli's pantry — it already knows what you have, when it expires, and what's most urgent.
2. Tap Rescue Chef.
3. Apple Intelligence reads the pantry, ranks ingredients by expiry urgency, and writes 3 recipes in 0.8 seconds.
4. Each recipe is grounded in your actual stock — no missing ingredients, no inventions.
