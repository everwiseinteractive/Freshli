YOUR KITCHEN. YOUR CLIMATE. TRACKED.

Food waste accounts for 8–10% of global greenhouse gas emissions. If food waste were a country, it would be the third-largest emitter on Earth — after only the US and China. Freshli puts that fight in your pocket.

LIVE RESCUE WAVE
Watch the world rescue food in real-time. Every item logged by every Freshli user, anywhere on Earth, animates across a global mesh-gradient map. Be part of the wave that hit 1 million rescues — and counting.

MEASURED BY SCIENCE
