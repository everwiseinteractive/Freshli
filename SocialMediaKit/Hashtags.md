## Hashtags to AVOID

These look adjacent but are wrong for us:

- `#vegan` / `#veganlife` — we're not vegan-only and the community is protective of brand-tagging
- `#mealkit` / `#hellofresh` — confuses the category
- `#weightloss` — actively off-brand (Brand Guide §9)
- `#foodporn` — wrong tone
- `#OOTD` / random lifestyle tags — dilutes targeting
- Anything with `#ad` *unless* the post genuinely is sponsored
