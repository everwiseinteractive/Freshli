# Freshli — Brand Guide for Social Media

A short, opinionated guide for the Alderframe team. Read once, refer back when in doubt.

---

## 1. The one-line story

> **Freshli is the on-device Apple Intelligence app that helps people rescue food before it expires — saving money, cutting CO₂, and feeding neighbours.**

If a single tweet had to do the job, it's the line above.

---

## 2. Voice & tone

| Trait | What it sounds like | What it doesn't sound like |
|-------|---------------------|----------------------------|
| **Direct** | "Rescue food. Save the planet." | "Here at Freshli we believe that food waste is, like, a big issue." |
| **Earnest** | "We do this because the planet is on fire and dinner is in the fridge." | Snarky, eye-rolling, ironic |
| **Human** | "Your half-tomato deserves better." | Corporate, jargon-heavy |
| **Confident** | "Apple Intelligence in your kitchen. On-device. Always." | Hedged: "potentially", "we hope" |
| **British** | "Mate, you've got 23 things going off this week." | Forced American slang |

**Sentence rule:** if you can cut a word, cut it. If you can replace a word with a stronger one, do.

**Never use:** "leverage," "synergy," "ecosystem," "revolutionary," "disrupt," "game-changing." We are not a SaaS deck.

---

## 3. The three core stories

Every post should be a beat in one of these three drumbeats. If a post doesn't fit any of them, it's probably off-brand.

### 🌱 STORY 1 — Rescue food, save money
- Tone: practical, useful, slightly cheeky
- Hooks: "This week alone you binned £14 of food." / "Your spinach has 36 hours."
- CTA: download / open the app

### 🤖 STORY 2 — Apple Intelligence, in your kitchen
- Tone: premium, confident, technical-but-accessible
- Hooks: "Recipes in 0.84 seconds, on your iPhone, with zero cloud."
- CTA: feature exploration, premium awareness
- IMPORTANT: always emphasise **on-device** and **private**. This is our differentiator vs. ChatGPT/Whisk/Mealime.

### 🌍 STORY 3 — For the planet, with proof
- Tone: serious, evidenced, hopeful
- Hooks: "Food waste = 10% of global emissions." Always cite (Poore & Nemecek 2018, FAO 2024).
- CTA: tracking impact, joining the wave

## 4. Colour palette

**Primary**
- Freshli Green `#22C55E` — buttons, key text accents
- Header Green `#16A34A` — hero gradients, primary backgrounds
- Deep Green `#15803D` — leaf vein, brand depth

**Accents (use sparingly, one per asset max)**
- Amber `#F59E0B` — CTA highlights, money saved, warmth
- Teal `#14B8A6` — climate stats, water/CO₂, cool topics
- Apple Intelligence Gradient (`#FBBF24` → `#2DD4BF` → `#A78BFA`) — **only** for AI Chef / Apple Intelligence content

**Neutrals**
- Ink `#030213` — body text on light backgrounds
- White `#FFFFFF` — body text on dark mesh backgrounds
- Pure black `#0A0A0A` — phone-screen mockups only

**Surface gradients** (use the prebuilt mesh classes from `_src/social.css`)
- `mesh-green` — default for most posts
- `mesh-light` — for stat-heavy / minimal posts
- `mesh-amber` — for warmth, savings, holiday content
- `mesh-ai` — exclusively for AI Chef / Apple Intelligence content

## 5. Typography

| Use | Family | Weight | Notes |
|-----|--------|--------|-------|
| Display headlines | SF Pro Display | 900 (Black) | Tight tracking (`-0.04em`) |
| Body | SF Pro Text | 600 (Semibold) | Loose tracking |
| Labels / eyebrows | SF Pro Text | 700 (Bold) | Wide tracking (`0.16em`), all caps |
| Stats / big numbers | SF Pro Display | 900 (Black) | Very tight tracking (`-0.06em`) |

If SF Pro is unavailable on the platform you're targeting (Canva, etc.), use **Inter** as the fallback — it has the closest weight ladder.

## 6. The leaf logo

The leaf is the brand. Treat it well.

- **Minimum size:** 32×32 px on screen (we lose the vein detail below that)
- **Clearspace:** keep the leaf's height of empty space around it on every side
- **Backgrounds:** the green leaf works on any light or mid-tone surface. Use the **white leaf with green vein** version on photos or dark mesh backgrounds.
- **Don't:** rotate, recolour to non-brand greens, drop-shadow excessively, or pair with another logo without 24px of separation.

## 7. Do / Don't quick reference

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|-------|---------|
| Use real product screenshots when possible | Use stock photos of "happy people in kitchens" |
| Cite climate statistics (Poore & Nemecek, FAO) | Make up numbers or pull from "11 fascinating facts" listicles |
| Lead with the verb: "Rescue", "Track", "Save" | Lead with "Introducing…" or "Discover…" |
| Use the Apple Intelligence gradient *only* for AI features | Slap the gradient on everything to look fancy |
| Mention privacy + on-device for AI content | Compare us to ChatGPT — we're not the same category |
| Tag `@freshli` and use `#FreshliApp` | Use the deprecated `#FreshliFood` |
| Reply to every comment in the first 4 hours | Auto-DM new followers |

## 8. Audience-by-platform tone

- **Instagram** — earnest, beautiful, ratio of 70% feed posts / 30% stories. Heavy on Weekly Wraps + AI Chef demos.
- **TikTok** — fast, useful, 15-second value drops. "3 things in your fridge dying right now." Demo over polish.
- **X / Twitter** — drier, sharper, climate science threads. Reply to climate journalists, food waste researchers, Apple writers.
- **LinkedIn** — founder voice, B2B-adjacent (sustainability teams, schools, councils). One post per week, long-form.
- **Threads** — conversational, behind-the-scenes, build-in-public energy. Daily check-ins.
- **YouTube** — long demos of AI Chef + impact dashboards. Not a priority for v1 — repurpose Reels.

---

## 9. The "no" list

Things we don't do, ever:

- Greenwashing claims ("100% sustainable", "carbon-neutral" — only what we can prove)
- Comparing users ("are you an A or B saver?" — gamification yes, judgement no)
- Diet content ("lose weight by saving food" — not the brand)
- Influencer #ad without `#ad` or `#sponsored` clearly disclosed
- Politics, beyond climate science — climate is in our remit, partisan content is not
- Using AI-generated photography of food (it dates instantly and audiences can spot it)
